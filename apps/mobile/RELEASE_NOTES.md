## PayTsek V2 beta — 0.2.5

- Payer names from wallet notifications are now always masked on your phone before anything is uploaded, even when a wallet shows the full name.
- Wallet notification recognition covers more real GCash, Maya, GoTyme, and MariBank formats (peso-sign amounts, InstaPay reference numbers, and more), verified by an expanded on-device test suite.
- Smaller reliability polish across scanning and syncing.

Wallet detection and notification matching are supporting evidence, not confirmation from a bank or wallet provider.
