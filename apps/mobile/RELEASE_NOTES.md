## PayTsek V2 beta

- A new five-destination navigation bar keeps Scan prominent while adding Analytics and Settings.
- Analytics summarizes recorded amounts by day, payment source, and evidence status.
- Records can be filtered by evidence and payment source, and searched by wallet or amount.
- GCash, Maya, GoTyme, and MariBank artwork now makes sources easier to recognize.
- Update prompts now show patch notes and can open Android's installer directly inside PayTsek.
- Sign-in, offline draft sync, notification matching, light/dark themes, and loading behavior were refreshed for the public beta.

Payment records remain saved even when notification evidence is unavailable. A “Strong match” is supporting evidence from the Android notification on the paired payment phone; it is not confirmation from a wallet provider.
