## PayTsek V2 beta — 0.2.2

- Fixed update downloads that could hand an incomplete APK to Android, and made longer patch notes scrollable.
- GCash, GoTyme, Maya, and MariBank notification listening can now be turned on or off directly in Settings.
- Wallet status now shows whether Android access is ready or recognized notifications are waiting to sync.
- Local and synced payment records can be opened and corrected, with retry controls for records waiting to upload.
- Removed the first-scan setup page, redundant empty-state actions, and the extra selected-navigation dot.
- New workspaces now open directly on Today while Scan remains the distinct center action.

Notification matching remains supplementary evidence. When a notification has no shared receipt reference, PayTsek shows a possible match for review instead of claiming provider verification.
